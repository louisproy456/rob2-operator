How to do the test
  1. Tablet and phone on the same home Wi-Fi.
  2. Tablet: open this page in Chrome, press CAMERA, type the EMQX login, press START CAMERA and allow the camera.
  3. Phone: open this page, press VIEWER, type the EMQX login, press WATCH. The picture appears in 1 to 3 seconds.
  4. Delay test: point the tablet's camera at the phone's screen so that it sees the big clock. On the phone you now see the real clock and, in the picture, an older clock. Press Freeze the picture and type the clock you read in the picture.
  5. Let it run 5 minutes, then press Copy report on both devices.
  6. Both jobs test: on the tablet put Chrome and the app "final1" in split screen, drive the robot, and look at "What limits the quality".
